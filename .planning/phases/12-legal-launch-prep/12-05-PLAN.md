<task type="checkpoint:human-action" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 3: Create App Preview video (screen recording)</name>
  <action>
Create 15-30 second screen recording showing app in action.

**CRITICAL: Apple requires actual screen recordings only. Do NOT create animated promotional content - it will be rejected.**
  </action>
  <instructions>
**Video Requirements (Apple 2026):**
- Duration: 15-30 seconds
- Resolution: 1290 x 2796 pixels (iPhone 16 Pro Max)
- Format: H.264 (.mov, .m4v, .mp4) or ProRes 422 HQ
- Content: ONLY what appears in the app - no overlays, hands, or simulated UI
- Audio: Can include app sounds or music (optional)

**Storyboard (aim for 25 seconds):**

0-5s: Video grid with Playbooks visible
- Show scrolling through saved videos
- Tap on a video

5-12s: Summary display
- Show bullet summary loading
- Scroll through key points
- Switch to step checklist (Pro feature)

12-18s: Habit extraction
- Tap "Action Points" button
- Show AI extracting habits
- Select a habit to create

18-25s: Habit tracking
- Show habit list with streaks
- Complete a habit
- Show streak update

**Recording Process:**
1. Open Xcode > Window > Devices and Simulators
2. Select iPhone 16 Pro Max simulator
3. Click "Record" button in simulator window
4. Perform the actions above smoothly
5. Stop recording
6. Trim to 25 seconds in QuickTime or iMovie
7. Export as H.264 .mp4

**Tips:**
- Practice the flow before recording
- Keep movements smooth and deliberate
- Pause briefly on important features
- Use sample data that looks realistic

Save to: `.planning/app-store/preview-video/`
  </instructions>
  <resume-signal>Type "video-ready" when app preview video is recorded and saved</resume-signal>
</task>
