<task type="checkpoint:human-action" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 1: Complete App Store Connect legal and banking setup</name>
  <action>
Complete the App Store Connect legal and banking requirements. This enables paid app distribution.
  </action>
  <instructions>
1. **Sign in to App Store Connect:** https://appstoreconnect.apple.com

2. **Sign Paid Applications Agreement:**
   - Go to: Agreements, Tax, and Banking
   - Find "Paid Applications" agreement
   - Click "Review" and accept terms
   - Status should change from "New" to "Active"

3. **Add Tax Information:**
   - In Agreements, Tax, and Banking > Tax Forms
   - Complete tax form for each relevant jurisdiction
   - For UK: Complete W-8BEN-E (if sole proprietor) or W-8BEN (individual)
   - Status should show "Complete" or "Clear"

4. **Add Banking Information:**
   - In Agreements, Tax, and Banking > Banking
   - Add bank account for each currency you want to receive payments in
   - Minimum: GBP account (for UK jurisdiction)
   - Status should show "Clear"

5. **Verify Completion:**
   - Paid Applications row should show status: "Active"
   - All tax forms should show: "Complete"
   - Banking should show: "Clear"
  </instructions>
  <resume-signal>Type "legal-complete" when Paid Applications Agreement is active and banking is set up</resume-signal>
</task>

<task type="checkpoint:human-action" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 2: Create subscription product</name>
  <action>
Create the Scrollsmith Pro subscription product in App Store Connect.
  </action>
  <instructions>
1. **Navigate to Subscriptions:**
   - App Store Connect > My Apps > Scrollsmith
   - Features tab > Subscriptions (under In-App Purchases)

2. **Create Subscription Group:**
   - Click "+" to create new subscription group
   - Reference Name: "Scrollsmith Pro" (internal only)
   - Click Create

3. **Create Subscription Product:**
   - Within the group, click "+" to create subscription
   - Reference Name: "Scrollsmith Pro Monthly"
   - Product ID: `scrollsmith_pro_monthly` (permanent, can't reuse if deleted)
   - Click Create

4. **Configure Subscription Details:**
   - Duration: 1 Month
   - Grace Period: None (or 7 days if desired)

5. **Set Pricing:**
   - Subscription Prices > Click "+"
   - Base Country: United Kingdom
   - Choose price tier (e.g., Tier 3 = £2.99/month or Tier 5 = £4.99/month)
   - Apple auto-calculates prices for all other countries
   - Save

6. **Add Localization:**
   - Subscription Information section
   - Display Name: "Scrollsmith Pro"
   - Description: "Unlimited videos, all summary formats, habit tracking"
   - Save

7. **Add Review Screenshot:**
   - Review Information section
   - Upload screenshot showing subscription benefits (can be placeholder for now)
   - Minimum size: 640 x 920 pixels

8. **Verify Status:**
   - Product should show status: "Ready to Submit"
   - If "Missing Metadata", check: pricing, localization, review screenshot

Note: Products take 2-6 hours to propagate to RevenueCat and StoreKit. Create early.
  </instructions>
  <resume-signal>Type "subscription-ready" when product status shows "Ready to Submit"</resume-signal>
</task>

<task type="checkpoint:human-action" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 3: Complete App Privacy details</name>
  <action>
Complete the App Privacy questionnaire for privacy nutrition labels.
  </action>
  <instructions>
1. **Navigate to App Privacy:**
   - App Store Connect > My Apps > Scrollsmith
   - App Privacy section (in left sidebar)

2. **Answer Privacy Questionnaire:**

   **Data Collection: YES** (the app collects data)

   **Data Types Collected:**

   a) **Contact Info > Email Address**
      - Collected: Yes
      - Linked to User: Yes
      - Used for Tracking: No
      - Purpose: App Functionality (account management)

   b) **Identifiers > User ID**
      - Collected: Yes (internal user ID)
      - Linked to User: Yes
      - Used for Tracking: No
      - Purpose: App Functionality

   c) **Usage Data > Product Interaction**
      - Collected: Yes (via Mixpanel)
      - Linked to User: Yes
      - Used for Tracking: No
      - Purpose: Analytics

   d) **Diagnostics > Crash Data**
      - Collected: Yes (via Sentry)
      - Linked to User: No
      - Used for Tracking: No
      - Purpose: App Functionality (error reporting)

   e) **Purchases > Purchase History**
      - Collected: Yes (via RevenueCat)
      - Linked to User: Yes
      - Used for Tracking: No
      - Purpose: App Functionality (subscription management)

   **Third-Party SDKs to disclose:**
   - RevenueCat: Purchase History, User ID
   - Sentry: Crash Data, Diagnostics
   - Mixpanel: Usage Data, User ID

3. **Privacy Policy URL:**
   - Enter: https://backend-production-d73a.up.railway.app/privacy

4. **User Tracking:**
   - Does this app request permission to track users? **No**
   - (We use analytics but don't track across apps/websites)

5. **Save and Publish:**
   - Review all entries
   - Click Publish
  </instructions>
  <resume-signal>Type "privacy-complete" when App Privacy shows all sections complete</resume-signal>
</task>
