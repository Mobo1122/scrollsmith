Add two GET endpoints to main.py to serve the legal documents:

```python
from fastapi.responses import FileResponse

@app.get("/terms")
async def terms_of_service():
    """Serve Terms of Service HTML page."""
    return FileResponse("app/static/legal/terms.html", media_type="text/html")

@app.get("/privacy")
async def privacy_policy():
    """Serve Privacy Policy HTML page."""
    return FileResponse("app/static/legal/privacy.html", media_type="text/html")
```

Add FileResponse import at top of file. Place routes after the root endpoint and before any other routers.

Note: These endpoints are at root level (not /api/v1/) because they are public-facing legal pages that iOS will display in WebView.
