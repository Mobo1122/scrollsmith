10. Verify Capture plus button is accessible
11. Tap Capture - should open CaptureView as fullScreenCover
12. Dismiss CaptureView - should return to previous view

**Critical checks (from research pitfalls):**
- Sidebar items should highlight AND change detail view (not just highlight)
- No freezing or stuck navigation states
