Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27 January 2026

Scrollsmith ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App") and related services.

This policy is designed to comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data Controller

Scrollsmith is the data controller responsible for your personal data. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

Email: hello@scrollsmith.app

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address and authentication credentials when you create an account.
  • Video Content: Videos you upload from your camera roll for transcription and summarisation.
  • Video URLs: Links to YouTube videos you submit for caption extraction.
  • User Preferences: Your playbook organisation, habit settings, and notification preferences.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the App, including features used, videos processed, and session duration.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
  • Crash Reports: Technical information about app crashes to help us improve stability.

3. How We Use Your Data

3.1 Core Service Delivery

We process your data to provide the App's core functionality:

  • Authenticating your account and maintaining your session;
  • Storing and organising your saved videos and summaries;
  • Delivering notifications for habit reminders.

3.2 AI-Powered Processing

Scrollsmith uses artificial intelligence and large language models (LLMs) to process your content. This is a core feature of our service:

Processing Type Technology Used What Happens
Video Transcription OpenAI Whisper API Audio from your uploaded videos is sent to OpenAI's servers for speech-to-text conversion.
Content Summarisation Anthropic Claude Your transcripts are sent to Anthropic's servers to generate summaries, action steps, and habit suggestions.

Important information about AI processing:

  • Your data is not used to train AI models. Both OpenAI and Anthropic have committed that data sent via their APIs is not used to train their models.
  • Video files are deleted from our servers immediately after transcription is complete.
  • Transcripts and summaries are stored in your account and retained while your account is active.
  • Legal basis: We process your data for AI-powered features based on legitimate interests (providing the core service you signed up for) under Article 6(1)(f) of UK GDPR.

3.3 Service Improvement

We use analytics data to understand how users interact with the App and to improve our services. This processing is based on our legitimate interests in operating and improving the App.

We share your data with the following third-party service providers who process data on our behalf:

5. Data Retention

  • Video Files: Deleted immediately after transcription is complete. We do not store your original video files.
  • Transcripts and Summaries: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Analytics Data: Anonymised data may be retained indefinitely for statistical purposes.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request that we delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restriction: You can request that we restrict the processing of your data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@scrollsmith.app. We will respond to your request within one month.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS;
  • Encryption of data at rest;
  • Regular security assessments;
  • Access controls limiting who can access your data.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information.
